\begin{abstract}
We present \BLINCS{}, a $100\%$ stateless hash-based signature scheme based on \BLAKE{} and SPHINCS sized for an operational lifetime budget of $q_s \le 2^{48}$ signatures per signing key ($2.81 \times 10^{14}$ messages; $128$-bit classical single-user security target). Pairing a $6$-layer hypertree ($13 + 7{\times}5 = 48$) of zero-coding-table bit-packed constant-sum \WOTSC{} one-time signatures ($n = 21, w = 60, z = 2, S = 679$, $u24$ counter) with a single height-$11$ \UPORS{} Octopus few-time tree ($n = 3, w = 24, S = 42, C = 376, k = 9, m_{\max} = 45$) whose codeword extraction \texttt{FEW\_CODE} is chained directly from the $32$-byte \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} digest $H_{\mathrm{sel}}$, \BLINCS{} achieves fixed-length \textbf{$3{,}990$-byte signatures} ($5{,}320$~B Base64---$49.2\%$ smaller than NIST FIPS~205 \textsf{SLH-DSA-128s}), $32$-byte verifying keys, pure $32$-byte \BLAKE{} everywhere (zero XOF mode), zero coding lookup tables, length-framed application contexts, and single-pass salted content hashing.

Our primary analytical contribution resolves how Octopus sibling capping affects few-time signature security: we prove that conditioning honest \UPORS{} histories on $\calF = \{S \subset [2048] : |S| = 9,\ \mathrm{sibs}(S) \le 45\}$ \emph{destroys negative association} and induces $30.35\times$ adjacent-sibling clustering, invalidating the usual marginal-product shortcut. Rather than relying on Monte Carlo sampling, we certify an all-integer $3$-term exponential upper envelope $u_3(t) \ge q_\calC(t)$ ($\forall t \ge 0$), enumerate \emph{all} $1{,}614{,}132$ isomorphism classes of $\calF$ modulo $\mathrm{Aut}(T_{11})$ (partitioning all $|\calF| = 180{,}700{,}154{,}447{,}569{,}471{,}488$ subsets), evaluate the exact binomial upper-envelope moments across every orbit in $256$-bit fractional arithmetic, and certify the final outward-rounded table in exact arbitrary-precision \texttt{BigInt} rational interval arithmetic (\texttt{tools/upors\_cert.rs}, zero floating-point reliance in the final bounds). This proves a single-raw-candidate upper-envelope bound of $P_{\mathrm{UB}}(48, 2^{48}) \le 2.2493 \times 10^{-40}$ ($\ge 131.7076\text{ bits}$). We further provide a $4$-game single-user classical ROM+PRF EUF-CMA reduction using exact query-trace accounting and an adaptive presampled marked-history coupling, a $6$-module Lean~4.36.0 formalization ($37$ theorems verified against a strict core-axiom allowlist), and cross-architecture evaluation across single-core Apple~M6 SIMD ($8.45\text{ ms}$ warm sign, $62.7\;\mu\text{s}$ verify, $27.1\text{ KiB}$ zero-import WASM) and bare-metal QEMU ARM Cortex-M33 emulation ($1{,}816\times$ fewer \WOTSC{} decoding instructions and $988\text{ B}$ verification stack watermark vs.\ zero-table Sperner DP).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Stateless hash-based signatures~\cite{bernstein2015sphincs,bernstein2019sphincsplus,nist2024fips205} base unforgeability solely on symmetric hash assumptions, avoiding elliptic-curve and lattice assumptions as well as the state-rollback hazards of stateful schemes~\cite{buchmann2011xmss,shrincs2025}. Their primary deployment cost has been signature size and signing latency: NIST FIPS~205 \textsf{SLH-DSA-128s} produces $7{,}856$-byte signatures ($10{,}476$~B Base64), while \textsf{SLH-DSA-128f} requires $17{,}088$~bytes.

For network services, software supply chains, and long-lived infrastructure, an operational signing budget must also be large enough to resist inadvertent signing-oracle exhaustion (``milking''). \BLINCS{} targets $q_s \le 2^{48}$ signatures per signing key ($281.5$~trillion signatures: one million per second nonstop for $8.9$~years) at a \emph{128-bit classical single-user security target} (multi-user and QROM proofs are explicitly open; see \cref{sec:quantum}).

\paragraph{Positioning and novelty.}
Constant-sum one-time signatures~\cite{bos1992csum,kudinov2022wotsplusc,zhang2023csum,khovratovich2025hypercube}, Octopus multi-proofs~\cite{aumasson2017gravity}, and forced-pruning few-time signatures (\textsf{PORS+FP}~\cite{abri2025porsfp}, \textsf{BPORS+FP}~\cite{ren2026bporsfp}, \textsf{BPORSC}~\cite{wang2026bporsc}) are each well-studied individually. What distinguishes \BLINCS{} is the combination of three concrete technical contributions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Zero-coding-table packed \WOTSC{} + chained \UPORS{} Octopus (\cref{sec:construction}):}
    A $6$-layer $13{+}7{\times}5$ hypertree ($h = 48$) using an exact $2 + 21 \cdot 6 = 128$-bit packed constant-sum slice $\calL_{679}(21, 60)$ (zero division, zero coding lookup tables, $< 1\text{ KiB}$ embedded stack) paired with a height-$11$ \UPORS{} tree of $3$-chain single-block ($64$-byte) leaves whose \texttt{FEW\_CODE} derivation is chained directly from the $32$-byte \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} digest $H_{\mathrm{sel}}$, forcing any code-first pre-query for a selection candidate to guess $H_{\mathrm{sel}}$ at zero cost while achieving fixed-length \textbf{$3{,}990$-byte signatures}.
  \item \textbf{Deterministic all-orbits rational-interval certification (\cref{sec:upors-security}):}
    Forced-pruning analyses such as \textsf{PORS+FP}~\cite{abri2025porsfp}, \textsf{BPORS+FP}~\cite{ren2026bporsfp}, and \textsf{BPORSC}~\cite{wang2026bporsc} analyze subset-pruned or bucket-thinned histories whose conditioning differs from full-tree Octopus sibling capping over chain-valued openings. We prove that conditioning honest histories on $\calF = \{S \subset [2048] : |S| = 9,\ \mathrm{sibs}(S) \le 45\}$ destroys negative association~\cite{joagdev1983na} ($30.35\times$ adjacent-sibling clustering), and evaluate a deterministic upper-envelope bound \emph{without Monte Carlo} by certifying an all-integer $3$-term exponential upper envelope $u_3(t) \ge q_\calC(t)$, summing across all $1{,}614{,}132$ $\mathrm{Aut}(T_{11})$-orbits in $256$-bit fractional arithmetic, and verifying the final binomial table in exact \texttt{BigInt} rational interval arithmetic (\texttt{tools/upors\_cert.rs}).
  \item \textbf{Game-based ROM+PRF proof, Lean~4 formalization, and cross-architecture co-design (\cref{sec:security,sec:lean,sec:codesign,sec:comparison}):}
    We give a $4$-game single-user classical ROM+PRF reduction with exact $|V|$ accepted-digest collision accounting, query-trace hidden-node charging, and an adaptive presampled marked-history coupling; connect it to a $6$-module Lean~4.36.0 formalization ($37$ strict-allowlist axiom checks, normative bounded minimum-counter signer exporting all $6$ layer minimalities, and exact Octopus sibling conservation); and report Apple~M6 benchmarks alongside auditable QEMU Cortex-M33 instruction/stack measurements and byte-only projections at $q_s = 2^{64}$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Primitive, Tweaks, and Single-Block Alignment}
\label{sec:prelim}

\BLINCS{} uses only two standard $32$-byte-output \BLAKE{} modes~\cite{oconnor2020blake3} and \emph{never} invokes \BLAKE{}'s extended-output (XOF) mode:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item $\Keyed(K, X) \in \bits^{256}$: \BLAKE{} \texttt{KEYED\_HASH} with $32$-byte key $K$.
  \item $\Derive(\mathsf{ctx}, X) \in \bits^{256}$: \BLAKE{} \texttt{DERIVE\_KEY} with UTF-8 context string $\mathsf{ctx}$.
\end{itemize}
From a $32$-byte secret $\mathsf{seed}$, \emph{four} secret-seed derivations produce $K_{\mathrm{sec}}$, $K_{\mathrm{salt}}$, $K_R \in \bits^{256}$ and $\mathsf{pk\_seed} \in \bits^{128}$ (truncated from $32$~B) using four distinct suite-bound contexts (\texttt{"BLINCS-48-X 2026-11-26 ..."}). From the public verifying key $\mathsf{SignetId} = \mathsf{pk\_seed} \parallel \mathsf{root}$ ($32$~B), \emph{two} public derivations produce $K_{\mathrm{pub}} = \Derive(\mathsf{CTX\_PUB}, \mathsf{pk\_seed})$ and $K_{\mathrm{content}} = \Derive(\mathsf{CTX\_CONTENT}, \mathsf{pk\_seed} \parallel \mathsf{root})$.

Every tree, chain, counter-grinding, and message-binding call is domain-separated by a $16$-byte tweak:
\[
  \Tweak(\mathsf{kind}, \ell, \mathsf{tree}, \mathsf{leaf}, a, b)
  \;=\;
  \mathsf{kind}\;(1) \parallel \ell\;(1) \parallel \mathsf{tree}\;(6) \parallel \mathsf{leaf}\;(2) \parallel a\;(2) \parallel b\;(4),
\]
with little-endian integers and zero unused fields. Hypertree layers use $\ell \in \{0,\dots,5\}$; the \UPORS{} tree uses $\ell = \mathtt{0x80}$ ($128$). Define $\ShortHash(K, T, X) = \Keyed(K, T \parallel X)[0..16]$ (half-open byte ranges $a..b$ denote $[a, b)$ throughout). Eight distinct tweak kinds partition all hash calls (\cref{fig:tweak}): all seven hot-path calls fit inside a \emph{single} $64$-byte \BLAKE{} compression block, and every counter-grinding loop places its incrementing $u32$ counter on a $4$-byte word boundary.

\paragraph{Terminology.}
We distinguish the $16$-byte per-signature randomizer $R$ from the binomial leaf occupancy count $R_{\mathrm{occ}} \sim \mathrm{Bin}(q_s, 2^{-48})$ at a hypertree leaf address; $H_{\mathrm{sel}}$ denotes the $32$-byte \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} digest and $H_\ell \in \bits^{128}$ denotes the $16$-byte truncated \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_MSG} prefix. In our companion library \texttt{signet}, a signing key is a \texttt{Signet}, its $32$-byte verifying key a \texttt{SignetId}, and a signature an \texttt{Imprint}.

\subsection{Packed Constant-Sum \WOTSC{} with Shifted Upper-Half Slice}
\label{sec:wotsc}
To eliminate checksum chains without incurring the dynamic-programming cost of Sperner unranking~\cite{zhang2023csum,khovratovich2025hypercube} on microcontrollers, \BLINCS{} instantiates \WOTSC{}~\cite{kudinov2022wotsplusc} via direct $6$-bit packing with $n = 21$ chains, alphabet size $w = 60$ ($\mathsf{WOTS\_STEPS} = 59$, digits $d_i \in \{0,\dots,59\}$), $z = 2$ zero bits, and shifted upper-half constant sum $S = 679$ (above the midpoint $\lfloor 21 \cdot 59 / 2 \rfloor = 619$).

For a $16$-byte truncated digest $H_\ell \in \bits^{128}$, let $\mathsf{lo} = \mathrm{LE128}(H_\ell) \in [0, 2^{128})$. Because $z + 6n = 2 + 21 \cdot 6 = 128$ bits exactly, every bit of $H_\ell$ is used with zero discarded prefix entropy: $H_\ell$ is \emph{valid} ($H_\ell \in \calV_{\mathrm{wots}}$) if and only if
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:wotsc-valid}
  (\mathsf{lo} \bmod 4) = 0,
  \qquad
  d_i = \Big\lfloor \frac{\mathsf{lo}}{2^{2 + 6i}} \Big\rfloor \bmod 64 < 60 \quad (\forall i \in \{0,\dots,20\}),
  \qquad
  \sum_{i=0}^{20} d_i = 679.
\end{equation}
Condition \eqref{eq:wotsc-valid} uses only shifts, bitmasks, and integer additions---zero division, zero $128$-bit DP, and zero lookup tables. Moreover, $H_\ell \mapsto (d_0, \dots, d_{20})$ is a \emph{bijection} from $\calV_{\mathrm{wots}} \subset \bits^{128}$ onto the constant-sum antichain $\calL_{679}(21, 60) = \{\mathbf{d} \in \{0,\dots,59\}^{21} : \sum_i d_i = 679\}$, with explicit inverse $\mathbf{d} \mapsto \mathrm{LE128}^{-1}(4 \sum_{i=0}^{20} d_i 64^i)$ (both directions kernel-checked in Lean~4: \texttt{validWotsHash\_\allowbreak eq\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak digits\_\allowbreak eq}, \texttt{encodeWotsCDigits\_\allowbreak valid}, \texttt{wotsCDigit\_\allowbreak encodeWotsCDigits}, \texttt{encodeWotsCDigits\_\allowbreak wotsCDigit}, and \texttt{wotsC\_\allowbreak dominance}).

\begin{proposition}[Exact \WOTSC{} Cardinality, Verification Savings, and Exhaustion Margin]
\label{prop:wotsc}
Let $N(w, n, s) = |\calL_s(n, w)| = [x^s](1 + \cdots + x^{w-1})^n$. By inclusion--exclusion over $d_i < w$ (proved directly on \texttt{sliceCount} in Lean~4, \texttt{wots\_\allowbreak sperner\_\allowbreak cardinality}),
\begin{align*}
  |\calV_{\mathrm{wots}}| \;=\; N(60, 21, 679)
  &\;=\; \sum_{j=0}^{11} (-1)^j \binom{21}{j}\binom{699 - 60j}{20} \\
  &\;=\; 83{,}261{,}328{,}062{,}314{,}682{,}436{,}358{,}950{,}675{,}433{,}803 \;\approx\; 2^{116.0032},
\end{align*}
whereas $N(60, 21, 680) < 2^{116}$ and $N(59, 21, 609) < 2^{116}$ (adjacent capacity checks at fixed $n = 21$ and target capacity $2^{116}$). Hence:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item A uniform $16$-byte prefix $H_\ell$ passes \eqref{eq:wotsc-valid} with exact probability $p_{\mathrm{wots}} = |\calV_{\mathrm{wots}}| / 2^{128} \approx 1 / 4{,}086.920$.
  \item For a fixed node and physical address, a $3$-byte counter $\mathrm{ctr}_\ell \in [0, 2^{24})$ ($u24$ LE on the wire, zero-extended to $u32$ word $8$ of the $36$-byte \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_MSG} block) exhausts with probability $(1 - p_{\mathrm{wots}})^{2^{24}} < 2^{-5{,}923.1}$.
  \item Shifting $S$ from midpoint $619$ to $679$ cuts verification chain work from $620$ to an \emph{exact constant} $21 \cdot 59 - 679 = \mathbf{560}$ steps per layer (saving $360$ verify compressions across $6$ layers while keeping fixed-target matching probability at $2^{-128}$ per raw query and adding $+0.40$ bits of grinding).
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

At hypertree position $(\ell, \mathsf{tree}, \mathsf{leaf})$ signing child root $\mathsf{node} \in \bits^{128}$, the deterministic signer selects the \emph{smallest} $\mathrm{ctr}_\ell \in [0, 2^{24})$ such that
\[
  H_\ell = \ShortHash\big(K_{\mathrm{pub}},\, \Tweak(\mathtt{ONE\_TIME\_MSG}, \ell, \mathsf{tree}, \mathsf{leaf}, 0, 0),\, \mathsf{node} \parallel \mathrm{u32\_le}(\mathrm{ctr}_\ell)\big) \in \calV_{\mathrm{wots}},
\]
failing closed without wrapping if all $2^{24}$ values fail. Deterministic minimality ensures that any physical hypertree key signs its unique child root with a unique digit vector across the lifetime of the key (while verification accepts any valid counter). Each \WOTSC{} leaf verifying-key hash takes $16 + 21 \cdot 16 = 352$~bytes ($6$ \BLAKE{} blocks), and the $6$ hypertree layers have heights $13 + 7 + 7 + 7 + 7 + 7 = 48$ ($h_{\mathrm{top}} = 13$ gives $8{,}192$ top leaves; all five lower layers have uniform height $7$, $128$ leaves each).

\subsection{\UPORS{}: Single Height-$11$ Upper-Slice Octopus Tree with Chained \texttt{FEW\_CODE}}
\label{sec:upors}
At each of the $2^{48}$ hypertree addresses $\mathsf{leaf\_addr} \in [0, 2^{48})$, the few-time layer is a single binary Merkle tree of height $A = 11$ ($L = 2{,}048$ leaves). Each leaf holds $n = 3$ chains of $23$ steps ($w = 24$), so $\Tweak(\mathtt{ONE\_TIME\_KEY}) \parallel x_{23}^{(0)} \parallel x_{23}^{(1)} \parallel x_{23}^{(2)}$ is $16 + 48 = 64$~bytes (exact $1$ \BLAKE{} block). Codewords lie on the upper constant-sum slice $\calC = \calL_{42}(3, 24) = \{(a,b,c) \in \{0,\dots,23\}^3 : a+b+c = 42\}$ of cardinality $|\calC| = \binom{44}{2} - 3\binom{20}{2} = 376$ (Lean~4: \texttt{upors\_slice\_count}, \texttt{uporsCode\_antichain}). Every opened leaf requires a constant $3 \cdot 23 - 42 = 27$ verify steps, and lexicographic unranking over $\calL_{42}(3, 24)$ is a $24$-row bounded integer scan with row lengths $5, 6, \dots, 24, 23, 22, 21, 20$ requiring \textbf{$0$~B of static tables}.

Given $D_{\mathrm{seed}} \in \bits^{256}$ (\cref{sec:content-hash}):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{\texttt{FEW\_SELECT} (\texttt{SPEC.md §8.1}):} For $\mathrm{ctr}_{\mathrm{ft}} \in [0, 2^{32})$, compute
    \[
      H_{\mathrm{sel}} \;=\; \Keyed\!\big(K_{\mathrm{pub}},\, \Tweak(\mathtt{FEW\_SELECT}, \mathtt{0x80}, 0,0,0,0) \parallel D_{\mathrm{seed}} \parallel \mathrm{u32\_le}(\mathrm{ctr}_{\mathrm{ft}})\big).
    \]
    Scan up to $18$ consecutive $11$-bit chunks starting at bit $0$ (bits $0..198$) to collect the first $k = 9$ distinct leaves in $[0, 2048)$, sort ascending to $S$, and compute the Octopus~\cite{aumasson2017gravity} sibling count $m = \mathrm{sibs}(S)$. Reject if fewer than $9$ distinct leaves appear (exact failure probability $1 - \alpha = 2048^{-18}\sum_{i=1}^{8}(2048)_i \genfrac{\{}{\}}{0pt}{}{18}{i} \approx 1.437 \times 10^{-22} \approx 2^{-72.56}$) or if $m > m_{\max} = 45$. Extract $\mathsf{leaf\_addr} \in [0, 2^{48})$ from bits $198..246$ (disjoint from bits $0..198$; bits $246..256$ are unused). The deterministic signer picks the smallest valid $\mathrm{ctr}_{\mathrm{ft}}$ (failing closed if $u32$ exhausts; exact exhaustion probability $(1 - \alpha p_{\mathrm{cap}})^{2^{32}} < 2^{-652{,}459}$); the verifier checks only the encoded $\mathrm{ctr}_{\mathrm{ft}}$.
  \item \textbf{Chained \texttt{FEW\_CODE} (\texttt{SPEC.md §8.2}):} Given the accepted $32$-byte digest $H_{\mathrm{sel}}$, for $\mathsf{blk} \in [0, 2^{16})$, compute
    \[
      B_{\mathsf{blk}} \;=\; \Keyed\!\big(K_{\mathrm{pub}},\, \Tweak(\mathtt{FEW\_CODE}, \mathtt{0x80}, 0,0,\mathsf{blk}, 0) \parallel H_{\mathrm{sel}}\big) \quad (48\text{ B})
    \]
    and read $28$ consecutive $9$-bit chunks, accepting values $< 376$ with zero modulo bias until $9$ codeword indices are collected. Chaining \texttt{FEW\_CODE} directly from $H_{\mathrm{sel}}$ (rather than from $D_{\mathrm{seed}} \parallel \mathrm{ctr}_{\mathrm{ft}}$) reduces input length by $4$~B while binding codeword extraction to the selection output so any code-first pre-query for a candidate must guess $H_{\mathrm{sel}}$ (\cref{sec:security}). In the ideal model, $\Pr[\mathrm{Bin}(28j, 376/512) < 9]$ is $\approx 9.29 \times 10^{-7}$ ($2^{-20.04}$) for $j = 1$ block and $\approx 2.96 \times 10^{-20}$ ($2^{-64.87}$) for $j = 2$ blocks; both signer and verifier fail closed if $\mathsf{blk}$ would exceed $65535$.
  \item \textbf{Canonical Octopus padding (\texttt{SPEC.md §10.2}):} Emit the $m \le 45$ Octopus siblings bottom-up followed by $(45 - m) \cdot 16$ zero bytes ($720$~B fixed); verifiers MUST reject any non-zero trailing padding byte in constant time.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Two-Stage Single-Pass Salted Content Hashing}
\label{sec:content-hash}
To hash arbitrarily large messages $M$ ($|M| < 2^{64}$) in a single streaming pass, the signer draws $32$ fresh random bytes $\mathsf{draw}$ before reading $M$ and computes, with $\mathsf{framed\_ctx} = \mathrm{u8}(|\mathsf{ctx}|) \parallel \mathsf{ctx}$ ($|\mathsf{ctx}| \le 255$):
\begin{gather*}
  e = \Keyed(K_{\mathrm{salt}}, \mathsf{draw})[0..16], \qquad
  K_{\mathrm{msg}} = \Keyed(K_{\mathrm{content}},\, e \parallel \mathsf{framed\_ctx}), \\
  h_{\mathrm{msg}} = \Keyed(K_{\mathrm{msg}}, M), \qquad
  \mathsf{short} = e \parallel h_{\mathrm{msg}} \;\;(48\text{ B}), \\
  R = \Keyed(K_R,\, \mathsf{short} \parallel \mathsf{framed\_ctx})[0..16], \\
  D_{\mathrm{seed}} = \Keyed\!\big(K_{\mathrm{pub}},\, \Tweak(\mathtt{MESSAGE}) \parallel R \parallel \mathsf{root} \parallel \mathsf{short} \parallel \mathsf{framed\_ctx}\big).
\end{gather*}
\emph{Normative commitment rule (\texttt{SPEC.md §7}):} signers MUST NOT disclose $e$ or $K_{\mathrm{msg}}$ to a message submitter before $(\mathsf{ctx}, M)$ is committed. Because $K_{\mathrm{content}}$ depends only on $\mathsf{SignetId}$, $K_{\mathrm{msg}}$ is public once $e$ is published (a randomized hash family, not a MAC). If $\mathsf{draw}$ repeats across distinct $(\mathsf{ctx}_1, M_1) \neq (\mathsf{ctx}_2, M_2)$ while $\mathsf{seed}$ remains secret, \texttt{rng\_\allowbreak failure\_\allowbreak hedged\_\allowbreak fallback} proves that either $\mathsf{ctx}_1 = \mathsf{ctx}_2$ with a collision on the public $256$-bit-output hash $h_{\mathrm{msg}}$ (generic classical collision work $\approx 2^{128}$), or the inputs to the secret-keyed PRF $\Keyed(K_R, \cdot)$ are strictly distinct (preserving pseudorandomness of $R$ under the PRF game).

\section{Exhaustive All-Orbits Capped-History \UPORS{} Certification}
\label{sec:upors-security}

Enforcing the Octopus sibling cap $m_{\max} = 45$ on \emph{both} honest signers and verifiers alters the honest-history law. Rather than assuming negative association or estimating target expectations via Monte Carlo, this section proves the failure of negative association and evaluates a deterministic all-orbits upper-envelope bound across the \emph{entire} family $\calF$.

\subsection{Exact Enumeration of $\calF$ and Proof That the Cap Breaks Negative Association}
In the ideal-output model of \texttt{FEW\_SELECT}, bits $0..198$ ($18$ draws of $11$-bit leaves) and bits $198..246$ ($\mathsf{leaf\_addr}$) are disjoint. Conditioned on obtaining $9$ distinct leaves within $18$ draws (probability $\alpha = 1 - 1.437 \times 10^{-22}$), the sorted $9$-subset is uniform on $\binom{[2048]}{9}$ by full permutation symmetry of the draws. Conditioning further on $\mathrm{sibs}(S) \le 45$ therefore induces the \emph{exact} product distribution $\mathsf{leaf\_addr} \sim \Unif([0, 2^{48}))$ independently of $S \sim \Unif(\calF)$, where $\calF = \{S \subset [2048] : |S| = 9,\ \mathrm{sibs}(S) \le 45\}$. (Notice that tree-automorphism invariance alone would not prove uniformity across the $1{,}614{,}132$ orbits of $\calF$; uniformity on $\calF$ follows from conditioning the uniform distribution on $\binom{[2048]}{9}$.)

The bivariate subtree enumerator $F_h(x, y) = \sum_{S \subseteq [2^h]} x^{|S|} y^{\mathrm{sibs}_h(S)}$ satisfies $F_0(x, y) = 1 + x$ and
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:Fh}
  F_{h+1}(x, y) \;=\; 1 \;+\; 2y\,(F_h(x, y) - 1) \;+\; (F_h(x, y) - 1)^2.
\end{equation}
Exact $u128$ evaluation of \eqref{eq:Fh} in \texttt{tools/upors\_cert.rs} gives
\begin{align*}
  |\calF| &= \sum_{s=10}^{45} [x^9 y^s]\, F_{11}(x, y) = 180{,}700{,}154{,}447{,}569{,}471{,}488, \\
  p_{\mathrm{cap}} &= \frac{|\calF|}{\binom{2048}{9}} \approx 1.0529230957 \times 10^{-4} \approx 2^{-13.2133}
\end{align*}
($1 / p_{\mathrm{cap}} \approx 9{,}497.370$; $47.98\%$ of $\calF$ lies at $s = 45$, $25.40\%$ at $s = 44$, $13.23\%$ at $s = 43$, across $36$ nonempty strata $s \in [10..45]$). The raw per-counter acceptance probability is $\alpha p_{\mathrm{cap}} \le p_{\mathrm{cap}}$.

While tree-automorphism invariance keeps every single leaf's marginal probability at $p = 9 / 2048$, conditioning on $\calF$ \emph{destroys negative association}~\cite{joagdev1983na}:

\begin{proposition}[Failure of Negative Association under $\Unif(\calF)$]
\label{prop:na-fails}
Under $D \sim \Unif(\calF)$, the leaf indicators $\{\mathbf{1}[\ell \in D]\}_{\ell \in [2048]}$ cannot have nonpositive pairwise covariances; in particular, they are not negatively associated.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Let $n_j(D)$ be the number of occupied height-$j$ subtrees ($n_0 = 9$, $n_{11} = 1$). Level $j$ emits $2 n_{j+1} - n_j$ siblings, so $\mathrm{sibs}(D) = \sum_{j=0}^{10}(2 n_{j+1} - n_j) = 2 n_{11} - n_0 + \sum_{j=1}^{10} n_j = \sum_{j=1}^{10} n_j - 7$. Hence $D \in \calF$ implies $\sum_{j=1}^{10} n_j \le 52$. If every distinct pair $u \neq v$ satisfied $\Pr[u, v \in D] \le p^2$ ($p = 9/2048$), then inside each height-$j$ block every occupied leaf beyond the first contributes at least one same-block pair, giving $\mathbb{E}[n_j] \ge 9 - \frac{2048}{2^j}\binom{2^j}{2} p^2 = 9 - \frac{81(2^j - 1)}{4096}$. Summing just $j = 1,\dots,7$ yields $\mathbb{E}[\sum_{j=1}^7 n_j] \ge 63 - \frac{81 \cdot 247}{4096} > 58.11 > 52$, a contradiction.
\end{proof}

Extending the exact integer tree DP \eqref{eq:Fh} to fixed pairs $\{0, 2^{d-1}\}$ gives the exact pairwise ratios $\Pr_{D \sim \Unif(\calF)}[u,v \in D] / p^2$ across LCA heights $d = 1,\dots,11$:
\[
  30.35\times,\; 18.27\times,\; 11.17\times,\; 6.95\times,\; 4.43\times,\; 2.89\times,\; 1.94\times,\; 1.35\times,\; 0.97\times,\; 0.73\times,\; 0.55\times
\]
(relative to the marginal product $p^2 = (9/2048)^2$). At occupancy $r = 1$ with one historical signature $D_1 \sim \Unif(\calF)$, any raw candidate $S^*$ covering $D_1$ must satisfy $S^* = D_1$ (automatically cap-valid) and match all $9$ codewords by the antichain property of $\calC$, yielding exact raw-query cover probability (up to $\alpha \le 1$ and non-exhausted \texttt{FEW\_CODE}):
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:r1}
  P_{\mathrm{raw}}(1) \;\le\; \binom{2048}{9}^{-1} \cdot 376^{-9} \approx 3.879248 \times 10^{-48} \approx 2^{-157.4968},
\end{equation}
confirming zero net cap credit at $r = 1$.

\subsection{Integer-Certified $3$-Term Envelope and Exact All-Orbits Binomial Series}
Let $q_\calC(t)$ be the probability that a uniform $c^* \in \calC$ dominates the coordinatewise minimum of $t$ i.i.d.\ uniform codewords in $\calC$ ($q_\calC(0) = 0$). For each $v \in \calC$, let $A_i(v) = |\{d \in \calC : d_i > v_i\}|$ and $B_{ij}(v) = |\{d \in \calC : d_i > v_i \wedge d_j > v_j\}|$; because no $d \in \calC$ exceeds $v$ in all three coordinates simultaneously, for every integer $t \ge 1$,
\[
  1 - q_\calC(t) \;=\; \frac{D(t)}{376^{t+1}}, \qquad D(t) \;=\; \sum_{v \in \calC} \left(\sum_{i=0}^{2} A_i(v)^t - \sum_{0 \le i < j \le 2} B_{ij}(v)^t\right).
\]
This yields $q_\calC(1) = 1/376 \approx 0.002660$, $q_\calC(2) \approx 0.170004$, $q_\calC(3) \approx 0.336239$, $q_\calC(4) \approx 0.458771$, $q_\calC(5) \approx 0.547735$.

\begin{lemma}[All-Integer Certified $3$-Term Exponential Upper Envelope]
\label{lem:envelope}
Define $u_3(t) = 1 - \frac{7495}{5546}(\frac{79}{100})^t + \frac{1949}{5546}(\frac{20}{100})^t$. Then $u_3(0) = q_\calC(0) = 0$, $u_3(1) = q_\calC(1) = \frac{1}{376}$, and $u_3(t) \ge q_\calC(t)$ for \emph{every} integer $t \ge 0$ (with $u_3(2)/q_\calC(2) \approx 1.00370$ and $u_3(3)/q_\calC(3) \approx 1.00080$).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At $t = 0$ both sides vanish. For $t \in \{1, \dots, 16\}$, dividing both sides of $1 - q_\calC(t) \ge 1 - u_3(t)$ by $4^t$ yields the exact integer inequality
\[
  5546 \cdot 25^t \cdot D(t) \;\ge\; 376 \cdot 94^t \cdot (7495 \cdot 79^t - 1949 \cdot 20^t),
\]
verified with equality at $t = 1$ and non-strict inequality for $t \in \{2,\dots,16\}$ in exact $256$-bit integer arithmetic inside \texttt{tools/upors\_cert.rs}. For all $t \ge 16$, exactly $15$ codewords $v \in \calC$ have a zero coordinate, and for each such coordinate $371$ codewords satisfy $d_i > 0$, giving $1 - q_\calC(t) \ge \frac{15}{376}(\frac{371}{376})^t$. Since $\frac{371}{376} > \frac{79}{100}$ and $\frac{15}{376}(\frac{371}{376})^{16} > \frac{7495}{5546}(\frac{79}{100})^{16}$ (checked in exact $256$-bit integers), $1 - q_\calC(t) > \frac{7495}{5546}(\frac{79}{100})^t > 1 - u_3(t)$ holds for every integer $t \ge 16$.
\end{proof}

Fix target $S^* = \{s_1, \dots, s_9\} \in \calF$, let $p_{S^*}(A) = \Pr_{D \sim \Unif(\calF)}[D \cap S^* = A]$ ($512$ subsets $A \subseteq S^*$), and define $G_{S^*,\mu}(\mathbf{z}) = \sum_{A \subseteq S^*} p_{S^*}(A) \prod_{i \in A} z_i$. Writing $(z_0, z_1, z_2) = (1, \frac{79}{100}, \frac{20}{100})$ and $(c_0, c_1, c_2) = (1, -\frac{7495}{5546}, \frac{1949}{5546})$, \cref{lem:envelope} and independence \emph{across} the $R_{\mathrm{occ}} \sim \mathrm{Bin}(q_s, 2^{-h})$ ideal historical signatures (with zero independence or negative-association assumption across the $9$ coordinates $T_i$) yield the closed-form binomial upper-envelope mixture:
\begin{align}
\label{eq:exact-binom}
  \mathbb{E}_{R_{\mathrm{occ}}}[P_{\mathrm{raw}}(R_{\mathrm{occ}})]
  &\;\le\; p_{\mathrm{cap}} \cdot \mathbb{E}_{S^* \sim \Unif(\calF)}\!\left[
    \sum_{\mathbf{j} \in \{0,1,2\}^9} c_{\mathbf{j}}\, \Big(1 - 2^{-h}\big(1 - G_{S^*,\mu}(\mathbf{z}_{\mathbf{j}})\big)\Big)^{q_s}
  \right] \nonumber \\
  &\;=\; p_{\mathrm{cap}} \sum_{k=1}^{K_{\mathrm{pow}}} (-1)^k \binom{q_s}{k} 2^{-hk} \, \overline{M}_k \;+\; R_{K_{\mathrm{pow}}},
\end{align}
where $c_{\mathbf{j}} = \prod_{i=1}^9 c_{j_i}$, the $k = 0$ term vanishes identically because $\sum_{j=0}^2 c_j = u_3(0) = 0$, and the exact all-orbits $k$-th moment is
\[
  \overline{M}_k \;=\; \frac{1}{|\calF|} \sum_{S^* \in \calF} \sum_{\mathbf{j} \in \{0,1,2\}^9} c_{\mathbf{j}}\, \big(1 - G_{S^*,\mu}(\mathbf{z}_{\mathbf{j}})\big)^k.
\]
Crucially, for every $S^* \in \calF$ and $\mathbf{z} \in [0, 1]^9$, union bounding over the $9$ leaves gives $0 \le u_{\mathbf{j}} = 1 - G_{S^*,\mu}(\mathbf{z}_{\mathbf{j}}) \le 9 \cdot \frac{9}{2048} = \frac{81}{2048} < 0.04$. Whenever $(q_s - K_{\mathrm{pow}} - 1)\frac{81}{2048} < (K_{\mathrm{pow}} + 2) 2^h$ (checked directly for every evaluated $(h, q_s)$), each tuple's binomial tail alternates with strictly decreasing magnitude for $k \ge K_{\mathrm{pow}} + 1$, so by the Leibniz test,
\[
  |R_{K_{\mathrm{pow}}}| \;\le\; p_{\mathrm{cap}} \cdot \left(\sum_{\mathbf{j}} |c_{\mathbf{j}}|\right) \cdot \binom{q_s}{K_{\mathrm{pow}} + 1} 2^{-h(K_{\mathrm{pow}} + 1)} \left(\frac{81}{2048}\right)^{K_{\mathrm{pow}} + 1},
\]
with exact integer-verified coefficient $\ell_1$-norm bound $\sum_{\mathbf{j}} |c_{\mathbf{j}}| = (1 + \frac{7495 + 1949}{5546})^9 = (\frac{14990}{5546})^9 \approx 7{,}698.27 < \mathbf{7{,}700}$ ($14990^9 < 7700 \cdot 5546^9$ asserted in $u128$). At $K_{\mathrm{pow}} = 44$, the capped remainder satisfies $|R_{44}| < 2^{-299}$ at $\lambda = 1$, $< 2^{-205}$ at $\lambda = 8$, and $< 2^{-201}$ at $\lambda = 20$.

\paragraph{Exhaustive all-orbits kernel and exact rational-interval certification.}
Because $G_{S^*,\mu}$ depends on $S^*$ only up to $\mathrm{Aut}(T_{11})$, \texttt{tools/upors\_\allowbreak cert.rs} enumerates \emph{all} \textbf{$1{,}614{,}132$ $\mathrm{Aut}(T_{11})$-orbits} of $\calF$ (verifying $\sum_{\mathcal{O}} |\mathcal{O}| = |\calF|$), computes exact $u128$ joint counts $p_{S^*}(A)$ via memoized prefix-sum tree DP (cross-checked against unoptimized height-$11$ DP, against brute-force enumeration on $H = 4, K = 3, m_{\max} = 6$ with relative agreement $1.09 \times 10^{-14}$, and against the independent closed-form trinomial sum for uncapped $r = 2$ to $1.15 \times 10^{-16}$), and deduplicates depth-$1$ sibling pairs over the $19{,}682$ nonzero tuples ($\mathbf{j} \neq \mathbf{0}$, since $1 - G(\mathbf{1}) = 0$).

In the $256$-bit pure-fractional fixed-point kernel ($\varepsilon = 2^{-256}$): (i)~exact-floor ratio initialization incurs error $< \varepsilon$ per mask probability; (ii)~each Kronecker axis satisfies $E' \le 2E + \varepsilon$, giving error $\le 1{,}023\varepsilon$ after $9$ axes; (iii)~dropped lower diagonals in \texttt{mul\_fast} plus flooring incur $< 4\varepsilon$; and (iv)~with $|c_{\mathbf{j}}|/16 < 1$ and $\widehat{u}_{\mathbf{j}} \le 81/2048 + 1{,}023\varepsilon < 0.04$, the power recurrence satisfies $E' \le 0.04 E + 1{,}027$, preserving invariant $E \le 1{,}080\varepsilon$ across all $k = 1..44$. For extreme overuse, exact integer certification in \texttt{tools/upors\_cert.rs} verifies $P_{\mathrm{UB}}^{\mathrm{rat}}(48, q_{96}) \le 2^{-96} < P_{\mathrm{UB}}^{\mathrm{rat}}(48, q_{96}+1)$ at $q_{96} = 5{,}291{,}482{,}952{,}600{,}082$ ($\lambda \approx 18.7991$, $\approx 21.11\text{ Exabytes}$ of $3{,}990$-byte signatures; by monotonicity of true coverage, every $q \le q_{96}$ inherits the $\ge 96$-bit lower bound on the raw-query \UPORS{} cover exponent, which does not locate the exact crossing of the true probability or whole-scheme advantage).

\section{Single-User Classical ROM+PRF EUF-CMA Security Proof}
\label{sec:security}

\paragraph{Oracle model, interfaces, and query trace.}
We analyze single-user EUF-CMA security in the classical Random-Oracle + PRF model with explicit oracle interfaces:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item The four secret-seed derivations $\Derive(\mathsf{CTX}, \mathsf{seed}) \mapsto (K_{\mathrm{sec}}, K_{\mathrm{salt}}, K_R, \mathsf{pk\_seed})$ are modeled as a $4$-output PRF ($\Adv^{\mathrm{PRF}}_{\mathrm{KDF}}$); $\Keyed(K_{\mathrm{sec}}, \cdot)$, $\Keyed(K_{\mathrm{salt}}, \cdot)$, and $\Keyed(K_R, \cdot)$ are modeled as independent secret-keyed PRFs ($\Adv^{\mathrm{PRF}}_{K_{\mathrm{sec}}}, \Adv^{\mathrm{PRF}}_{K_{\mathrm{salt}}}, \Adv^{\mathrm{PRF}}_{K_R}$), with PRF distinguishers running in the time of $\mathcal{A}$ plus keygen and at most $q_s$ signing calls.
  \item All non-secret \BLAKE{} evaluations---$\Derive(\mathsf{CTX}, X)$ for any context string $\mathsf{CTX}$ and $\Keyed(K, X)$ for arbitrary $32$-byte keys $K$---are answered by a \emph{single} unified random oracle $\mathcal{O}(\mathsf{mode}, K, X) \in \bits^{256}$ indexed by mode, key/context bytes, and full input bytes. Every $\mathcal{O}$-query made by $\mathcal{A}$ and by the final verification call $\mathsf{Verify}(\mathsf{SignetId}, \mathsf{ctx}^*, M^*, \sigma^*)$ is recorded in an ordered query table $\mathcal{T}$ of size $|\mathcal{T}| \le Q_H$. Let $Q_{\mathrm{sel}} \le Q_H$ be the number of distinct fresh \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} queries in $\mathcal{T}$.
\end{itemize}

\begin{theorem}[Classical Single-User ROM+PRF EUF-CMA Bound for \BLINCS{}]
\label{thm:euf-cma-rom}
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be any classical EUF-CMA adversary making at most $q_s \le 2^{48}$ adaptive signing queries (with independent uniform $256$-bit $\mathsf{draw}_j \sim \Unif(\bits^{256})$, committing $(\mathsf{ctx}_j, M_j)$ before learning $e_j$ per \texttt{SPEC.md §7}) and at most $Q_H$ total whole-input $\mathcal{O}$-queries (including final verification). Let $V \subset \bits^{256}$ be the set of cap-valid \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} digests ($|V| = \alpha p_{\mathrm{cap}} 2^{256} \approx 2^{242.79}$), and let $P_{\mathrm{UB}}(48, q_s)$ be the certified single-raw-candidate upper-envelope bound of \cref{tab:itsr} ($P_{\mathrm{UB}}(48, 2^{48}) \le 2.2493 \times 10^{-40}$). Then
\begin{align}
\label{eq:euf-cma-rom}
  \Adv^{\mathrm{EUF\text{-}CMA}}_{\BLINCS}(\mathcal{A})
  \;\le\;&
    \Adv^{\mathrm{PRF}}_{\mathrm{KDF}} + \Adv^{\mathrm{PRF}}_{K_{\mathrm{sec}}} + \Adv^{\mathrm{PRF}}_{K_{\mathrm{salt}}} + \Adv^{\mathrm{PRF}}_{K_R}
    \;+\; \varepsilon_{\mathrm{exh}}(q_s) \nonumber \\
  &+\; \frac{4 Q_H}{2^{256}} + \frac{q_s(q_s - 1)}{2^{257}} + \frac{(Q_H + q_s + 5)^2}{2^{257}} + \frac{(Q_H + 2 q_s)^2}{2^{257}} \nonumber \\
  &+\; \frac{2 q_s Q_H}{2^{256}} + \frac{Q_H(Q_H + 2 q_s)}{2^{256}}
    \;+\; \frac{q_s(q_s - 1)}{2 \alpha p_{\mathrm{cap}} 2^{256}} + \frac{q_s Q_H}{\alpha p_{\mathrm{cap}} 2^{256}} \nonumber \\
  &+\; \frac{2 Q_H}{2^{128}}
    \;+\; Q_H \cdot P_{\mathrm{UB}}(48, q_s),
\end{align}
where $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{exh}}(q_s) \le 2^{49} (1 - p_{\mathrm{wots}})^{2^{24}} + q_s (1 - \alpha p_{\mathrm{cap}})^{2^{32}} + q_s \Pr[\mathrm{Bin}(28 \cdot 65536, 376/512) < 9] < 2^{-5{,}874}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
We construct an unconditioned ideal tape experiment and bound every discrepancy via first-conflict and first-hit couplings across four steps, starting from the real EUF-CMA game $\mathbf{G}_0$ with \texttt{signNormative}.

\emph{Step 1 (PRF hybrids and unconditioned ideal tapes):}
Replace $(\mathsf{seed} \mapsto (K_{\mathrm{sec}}, K_{\mathrm{salt}}, K_R, \mathsf{pk\_seed}))$ and $\Keyed(K_{\mathrm{sec}}, \cdot)$, $\Keyed(K_{\mathrm{salt}}, \cdot)$, $\Keyed(K_R, \cdot)$ by independent uniform strings and independent uniform random functions (cost $\Adv^{\mathrm{PRF}}_{\mathrm{KDF}} + \Adv^{\mathrm{PRF}}_{K_{\mathrm{sec}}} + \Adv^{\mathrm{PRF}}_{K_{\mathrm{salt}}} + \Adv^{\mathrm{PRF}}_{K_R}$). Before $\mathcal{A}$ begins, sample completely \emph{unconditioned}, mutually independent ideal tapes:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \emph{Honest signing tapes ($j \in \{1,\dots,q_s\}$):} an independent \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} output stream $(Y_{j, 0}, Y_{j, 1}, \dots) \stackrel{\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim} \Unif(\bits^{256})$ (defining the first valid index $c_j^*$, the accepted digest $H_{\mathrm{sel}, j} = Y_{j, c_j^*} \in V$, and the exact rejected prefix $Y_{j,0}, \dots, Y_{j, c_j^* - 1} \notin V$) paired with an independent \texttt{FEW\_CODE} block stream $(B_{j, 0}, B_{j, 1}, \dots) \stackrel{\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim} \Unif(\bits^{256})$ (defining all rejection positions, unused bits, and the $9$-codeword vector $C_j$). Conditional on non-exhaustion of the ideal streams, slot $j$ defines an i.i.d.\ ideal marked triple $(a_j, D_j, C_j) \sim \Unif([2^{48}]) \times \Unif(\calF) \times \Unif(\calC^9)$ forming the full list $\mathcal{H} = ((a_1, D_1, C_1), \dots, (a_{q_s}, D_{q_s}, C_{q_s}))$.
  \item \emph{Raw adversarial \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} trial tapes ($t \in \{1,\dots,Q_{\mathrm{sel}}\}$):} an independent raw digest $Z_t \stackrel{\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim} \Unif(\bits^{256})$ paired with an independent \texttt{FEW\_CODE} block stream $(W_{t, 0}, W_{t, 1}, \dots) \stackrel{\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim} \Unif(\bits^{256})$.
  \item \emph{Global physical \WOTSC{} tapes:} for each of the $\sum_{j=0}^{5} 2^{48 - 7j} < 2^{49}$ physical hypertree keys, its child root is fixed solely by tags $1$--$4$ (disjoint from tag $5$), independently of its $2^{24}$ tag-$5$ \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_MSG} digests.
\end{itemize}
In this unconditioned ideal tape space, define $\mathsf{Exh}$ as the union of any physical \WOTSC{} key failing $[0, 2^{24})$ or any honest slot $j \le q_s$ failing $[0, 2^{32})$ in $Y_j$ or $[0, 2^{16})$ in $B_j$. By union bound on the unconditioned tapes, $\Pr[\mathsf{Exh}] \le \varepsilon_{\mathrm{exh}}(q_s) < 2^{-5{,}874}$.

\emph{Step 2 (First-conflict coupling $\mathsf{Conflict}$ for consistent table programming):}
Run $\mathcal{A}$ while coupling $\mathcal{O}$ to the unconditioned tapes using a slot counter $s \in [0, q_s]$ (initially $0$) and a fresh raw-trial counter $r \in [0, Q_{\mathrm{sel}}]$ (initially $0$):
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item Whenever $\mathcal{A}$ queries the signing oracle on $(\mathsf{ctx}_i, M_i)$: if $(e_i, \mathsf{short}_i, R_i, D_{\mathrm{seed}, i})$ repeats an earlier signing query, replay that exact signature without advancing $s$ (revealing zero new leaves). Otherwise advance $s \gets s + 1$, program $(D_{\mathrm{seed}, s}, 0), \dots, (D_{\mathrm{seed}, s}, c_s^*)$ in $\mathcal{O}$ to $Y_{s, 0}, \dots, Y_{s, c_s^*}$, and program \texttt{FEW\_CODE}$(\mathsf{blk}, H_{\mathrm{sel}, s})$ in $\mathcal{O}$ to $B_{s, \mathsf{blk}}$ (subsequent reads by $\mathcal{A}$ of these programmed entries simply read the table and are \emph{never} conflicts!).
  \item Whenever $\mathcal{A}$ or $\mathsf{Verify}$ issues a \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} query on an \emph{unoccupied} input $(D, c)$, advance $r \gets r + 1$, return $Z_r$, and if $Z_r \in V$ program \texttt{FEW\_CODE}$(\mathsf{blk}, Z_r)$ to $W_{r, \mathsf{blk}}$ (again, later reads of programmed entries are ordinary table reads, never conflicts).
\end{itemize}
Define $\mathsf{Conflict}$ as the \emph{first} event during execution that would either cause a secret/public oracle inconsistency, overwrite an already-occupied table entry, or merge distinct \texttt{FEW\_SELECT}/\texttt{FEW\_CODE} streams:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \emph{Secret-key / seed guess or derived-key value coincidence:} Some query in $\mathcal{T}$ supplies $\mathsf{seed}$ to \texttt{DERIVE} or $K \in \{K_{\mathrm{sec}}, K_{\mathrm{salt}}, K_R\}$ to \texttt{KEYED} ($\le 4 Q_H / 2^{256}$); or any pair of distinct evaluations among $\{K_{\mathrm{sec}}, K_{\mathrm{salt}}, K_R, K_{\mathrm{pub}}, K_{\mathrm{content}}\}$ and all queried $K_{\mathrm{msg}}$ produces equal $256$-bit key bytes ($\le (Q_H + q_s + 5)^2 / 2^{257}$). Absent this event, the secret-keyed random functions and public $\mathcal{O}$ are $100\%$ consistent.
  \item \emph{Draw collision, pre-occupied \texttt{MESSAGE} input, $h_{\mathrm{msg}}/D_{\mathrm{seed}}$ collision, or pre-occupied \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} argument:}
    (a)~Two signing queries sample the same $256$-bit $\mathsf{draw}_i = \mathsf{draw}_j$: $\le q_s(q_s - 1) / 2^{257}$. Absent a draw collision, every signing query feeds a strictly new input into the random function $K_{\mathrm{salt}}$, producing a fresh independent $e_i \sim \Unif(\bits^{128})$. Since $(\mathsf{ctx}_i, M_i)$ is committed before $e_i$ is disclosed (\texttt{SPEC.md §7}), for every fixed prior \texttt{MESSAGE} query $(R', e', h', \mathsf{ctx}') \in \mathcal{T}$, $e_i$ matches $e'$ with probability $2^{-128}$, and conditional on $e_i = e'$ any non-replay $(\mathsf{ctx}_i, M_i)$ feeds a fresh input into $K_R$ (\texttt{rng\_\allowbreak failure\_\allowbreak hedged\_\allowbreak fallback}) so $R_i$ independently matches $R'$ with probability $2^{-128}$---total product $2^{-256}$ per pair, or $\le q_s Q_H / 2^{256}$ across all pairs.
    (b)~Distinct-input $256$-bit output collisions among $h_{\mathrm{msg}}$ or $D_{\mathrm{seed}}$ evaluations: $\le (Q_H + 2 q_s)^2 / 2^{257}$.
    (c)~Because each new honest $D_{\mathrm{seed}, s}$ is freshly sampled in $\bits^{256}$ absent (a)--(b), the probability it matches the $D$-component of any \emph{previously queried} \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} entry $(D_0, c)$ in $\mathcal{T}$ is $\le q_s Q_H / 2^{256}$.
  \item \emph{Time-ordered \texttt{FEW\_SELECT}/\texttt{FEW\_CODE} cross-hits (strictly against previously recorded entries):}
    (a)~Two distinct honest accepted digests $H_{\mathrm{sel}, j} = H_{\mathrm{sel}, j'} \in V$: $\le \frac{q_s(q_s - 1)}{2 \alpha p_{\mathrm{cap}} 2^{256}}$ ($\approx 2^{-147.8}$).
    (b)~A fresh honest accepted digest $H_{\mathrm{sel}, s} \sim \Unif(V)$ matching any \emph{previously recorded} valid \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} output $Z_{r'} \in V$ or \emph{previously queried} valid \texttt{FEW\_CODE} argument $H \in V$ in $\mathcal{T}$: $\le \frac{q_s Q_H}{\alpha p_{\mathrm{cap}} 2^{256}}$ ($\approx Q_H / 2^{194.8}$).
    (c)~A fresh raw adversarial \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} output $Z_r \sim \Unif(\bits^{256})$ matching any \emph{previously queried} \texttt{FEW\_CODE} argument, any \emph{previously generated} raw \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} output $Z_{r'}$ ($r' < r$, preventing raw--raw $Z_{r'} = Z_r \in V$ code-stream overwrites!), or any \emph{previously generated} honest accepted $H_{\mathrm{sel}, s}$: since prior \texttt{FEW\_CODE} queries and prior raw \texttt{FEW\_SELECT} queries together belong to $\mathcal{T}$ (numbering at most $Q_H$), plus at most $q_s$ prior honest accepted digests (total $\le Q_H + q_s \le Q_H + 2 q_s$ prior targets per query), summing over all $r \le Q_{\mathrm{sel}} \le Q_H$ gives $\le \frac{Q_H(Q_H + 2 q_s)}{2^{256}}$.
\end{enumerate}
Up to $\mathsf{Conflict} \vee \mathsf{Exh}$, the coupled experiment reproduces the exact real distribution of $\mathbf{G}_0$ (after PRF replacement) with zero table overwrites, and rules out Events~(1)--(3) of \texttt{blincs\_forgery\_dichotomy} (including selection--selection and raw--raw code-programming conflicts).

\emph{Step 3 (Path-trace extraction and online-frontier shadow bound for Events~(4)--(12)):}
Suppose $\mathcal{A}$ outputs a verifying forgery $(\mathsf{ctx}^*, M^*, \sigma^*)$ and $\neg (\mathsf{Conflict} \vee \mathsf{Exh})$ holds, while Event~(13) (\UPORS{} cover) does not occur. Walking the actual recorded verification trace $\mathcal{T}$ of $\sigma^*$ downward from $\mathsf{root}$ extracts an explicit offending query \emph{inside $\mathcal{T}$} at its exact physical coordinate (strengthening the existential witness of \texttt{blincs\_forgery\_dichotomy} directly on the verifier's own computation):
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item Across hypertree layers $\ell = 5 \to 0$: if the verifier's child pair at a \texttt{NODE} or endpoint vector at \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_KEY} first diverges from the honest physical subtree, that exact query is evaluated by $\mathsf{Verify}$ and lies in $\mathcal{T}$ at its valid physical coordinate $p < 2^{h_\ell - \mathsf{lvl}}$. Otherwise, all endpoints match: if $(m^*, c^*) \neq (m_{\mathrm{hon}}, c_{\mathrm{hon}})$ with $H_\ell^*[0..16] = H_\ell^{\mathrm{hon}}[0..16]$, that exact \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_MSG} query lies in $\mathcal{T}$; if $H_\ell^* \neq H_\ell^{\mathrm{hon}}$, \texttt{wotsC\_dominance} yields a chain $c$ with $d_c^* < d_c^{\mathrm{hon}} \le 59$ (strictly below endpoint $59$), and stepping forward through $\mathsf{Verify}$'s \texttt{CHAIN} calls locates the first merging step $s \in [d_c^*, 58]$ in $\mathcal{T}$ (either submitting the honest node $v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, s)$ for $s < d_c^{\mathrm{hon}}$ or producing a forward $128$-bit \texttt{CHAIN} collision).
  \item On the \UPORS{} tree: walking the Octopus climb locates either an evaluated \texttt{NODE} or \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_KEY} collision in $\mathcal{T}$; otherwise all $9$ leaves match their honest hashes, and since Event~(13) failed, some target leaf $u^*$ has an uncovered coordinate. If $u^*$ was \emph{never} opened in any signing query, explicitly reselect a coordinate $c \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ with $d_c^* \le \lfloor 42 / 3 \rfloor = 14 < 23$ (also uncovered); if $u^*$ was opened, the uncovered coordinate satisfies $d_c^* < s_{\min}(c) \le 23$. In both cases $d_c^* < 23$ is strictly below the endpoint, so stepping forward along $\mathsf{Verify}$'s \texttt{CHAIN} calls on chain $c$ locates a sub-endpoint hidden \texttt{CHAIN} input hit or a forward $128$-bit \texttt{CHAIN} collision inside $\mathcal{T}$---avoiding any endpoint-vector guessing event at \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_KEY}.
\end{itemize}
To bound this event without post-miss conditional inflation or hindsight-frontier inconsistency, construct an \emph{online-frontier non-branching shadow experiment} $\mathbf{G}_3^{\mathrm{sh}}$:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \emph{Pre-sampled honest tree labels:} Before $\mathcal{A}$ begins, sample every physical chain's nodes $v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, 0), \dots, v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, w{-}1)$ as independent uniform $128$-bit strings (equivalently, each chain start $v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, 0) \sim \Unif(\bits^{128})$ and each forward step $v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, s{+}1) \sim \Unif(\bits^{128})$ on the honest input), together with all honest \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_KEY}, Merkle \texttt{NODE}, and honest-child \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_MSG} streams, fixing the unique honest target $Y_{\mathrm{hon}}(T)$ at every tweak $T$.
  \item \emph{Online query-time sub-endpoint frontier $s_{\min}^{(r)}(c)$:} Track, for every physical chain $c$ (endpoint $w{-}1 \in \{59, 23\}$), the sub-endpoint frontier $s_{\min}^{(r)}(c) = \min(w{-}1,\, d_{\mathrm{discl}}^{(r)}(c))$, where $d_{\mathrm{discl}}^{(r)}(c)$ is the minimum step disclosed by signing queries strictly \emph{before} oracle query $q_r$ (or $w{-}1$ if $c$ has not yet been opened). Thus every step $s < s_{\min}^{(r)}(c)$ is strictly sub-endpoint ($s \le w{-}2$) and its label $v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, s)$ never appears in any \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_KEY}, \texttt{NODE}, or \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_MSG} input. Whenever a signing query opens chain $c$ at step $d < s_{\min}(c)$, reveal $v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, d)$ and its authentication path directly from the pre-sampled honest tree, lower $s_{\min}(c) \gets d$, and record the honest mappings for newly disclosed suffix steps $s \in [d, s_{\min}^{\mathrm{old}}(c))$ in the oracle table.
  \item \emph{Non-branching shadow oracle responses:} When $\mathcal{A}$ or $\mathsf{Verify}$ submits query $q_r = (\mathtt{KEYED}, K_{\mathrm{pub}}, T \parallel X)$: (i)~if $q_r$ repeats an earlier entry in $\mathcal{T}$, return the recorded answer; (ii)~honor from the outset \emph{all} planted honest \texttt{NODE}, \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_KEY}, and honest-child \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_MSG} mappings (at the honest child $m_{\mathrm{hon}}$: returning the invalid honest prefix for $c < c_{\mathrm{hon}}$, the accepted $H_\ell^{\mathrm{hon}}$ for $c = c_{\mathrm{hon}}$, and the stored independent post-accepted digest for $c > c_{\mathrm{hon}}$), as well as every currently disclosed honest \texttt{CHAIN} entry ($X = v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, s)$ with $s \ge s_{\min}^{(r)}(c)$); (iii)~otherwise ($q_r$ is a fresh query outside all mappings in (ii)), \emph{always} return a freshly sampled $U_r \stackrel{\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim} \Unif(\bits^{256})$ without inspecting or branching on any currently hidden sub-endpoint label $\{v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, s) : s < s_{\min}^{(r)}(c)\}$.
\end{itemize}
Define the first-discrepancy event $\mathsf{Hit}$ on $\mathbf{G}_3^{\mathrm{sh}}$: some fresh query $q_r \in \mathcal{T}$ triggers either (a)~a \texttt{CHAIN} query at coordinate $(c, s)$ with $s < s_{\min}^{(r)}(c)$ (strictly sub-endpoint and below the frontier \emph{at query time}) whose input $X$ equals $v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, s)$, or (b)~a fresh off-honest query (\texttt{NODE}, \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_KEY}, \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_MSG} with $m^* \neq m_{\mathrm{hon}}$ or $c^* > c_{\mathrm{hon}}$ [tested on its stored independent post-accepted digest], or \texttt{CHAIN} with $X \neq v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, s)$) whose fresh output $U_r[0..16]$ matches $Y_{\mathrm{hon}}(T)$. Up to the first query triggering $\mathsf{Hit}$, $\mathbf{G}_3^{\mathrm{sh}}$ is \emph{identically distributed} to the coupled real game: all planted honest \texttt{NODE}, \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_KEY}, and honest-child \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_MSG} inputs are honored identically from the outset, no pre-query ever guessed a hidden sub-endpoint $v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, s)$ prior to any later suffix release (so suffix programming never overwrites a prior answer), no off-honest query collided onto an honest target, and every repeat returns its recorded answer. Crucially, because all \texttt{NODE} and \texttt{ONE\_TIME\_KEY} mappings depend only on Merkle nodes and chain \emph{endpoints} $v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, w{-}1)$---which are independent of all sub-endpoint labels $\{v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, s) : s \le w{-}2\}$ in the pre-sampled tree---and $\mathbf{G}_3^{\mathrm{sh}}$ never branches on hidden sub-endpoint labels, at the instant query $q_r$ is submitted every currently hidden label $v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, s)$ ($s < s_{\min}^{(r)}(c)$) is uniform in $\bits^{128}$ conditioned on $\mathcal{A}$'s entire view prior to $q_r$, and $U_r$ is freshly drawn independently of the view and $Y_{\mathrm{hon}}(T)$. Hence $\Pr_{\mathbf{G}_3^{\mathrm{sh}}}[\text{(a) at } q_r \mid \mathrm{view}_{< r}] \le 2^{-128}$ (only for \texttt{CHAIN}) and $\Pr_{\mathbf{G}_3^{\mathrm{sh}}}[\text{(b) at } q_r \mid \mathrm{view}_{< r}] \le 2^{-128}$; taking expectations and summing over all $|\mathcal{T}| \le Q_H$ queries proves unconditionally $\Pr[\mathsf{Hit}] \le 2 Q_H / 2^{128}$. Finally, because every hidden chain witness extracted above for Events~(4)--(12) is strictly sub-endpoint ($s \le 58 < 59$ for \WOTSC{}, $s \le 22 < 23$ for \UPORS{}), it satisfies either (a) (at the first query submitting $v_{\mathrm{hon}}(c, s)$ before its disclosure, or if never disclosed) or (b), giving $\{\text{Events~(4)--(12)} \wedge \neg(\mathsf{Conflict} \vee \mathsf{Exh})\} \subseteq \mathsf{Hit}$.

\emph{Step 4 (Unconditioned ideal-tape inclusion for Event~(13)):}
Finally, suppose Event~(13) occurs and $\neg (\mathsf{Conflict} \vee \mathsf{Exh})$ holds. Because $\neg \mathsf{Conflict}$ precludes any table overwrite or stream collision, the forged signature's $(D_{\mathrm{seed}}^*, \mathrm{ctr}_{\mathrm{ft}}^*)$ was evaluated at some fresh raw trial index $t \in \{1, \dots, Q_{\mathrm{sel}}\}$ (replays of honest accepted queries repeat an honest message/context and cannot be a fresh forgery, and reads of rejected entries fail $V$), receiving the unconditioned ideal tape output $(Z_t, W_t)$. Meanwhile, the signing transcript revealed only a prefix of length $s_{\mathrm{end}} \le q_s$ of the unconditioned ideal list $\mathcal{H}$. By \emph{monotonicity of coverage} under completing the prefix to all $q_s$ entries of $\mathcal{H}$, we obtain the exact set inclusion with \emph{zero conditioning on the right-hand side}:
\[
  \{\text{Event~(13)} \wedge \neg \mathsf{Conflict} \wedge \neg \mathsf{Exh}\}
  \;\subseteq\;
  \bigcup_{t=1}^{Q_{\mathrm{sel}}}
    \big\{\text{unconditioned ideal trial } (Z_t, W_t) \text{ is covered by full } \mathcal{H}\big\}.
\]
For each fixed $t$, $(Z_t, W_t)$ is completely independent of the full $q_s$-entry ideal list $\mathcal{H}$, so \eqref{eq:exact-binom} and \cref{tab:itsr} give unconditional probability $\le \alpha P_{\mathrm{UB}}(48, q_s) \le P_{\mathrm{UB}}(48, q_s)$. Union bounding over all $Q_{\mathrm{sel}} \le Q_H$ trial indices yields $\Pr[\text{Event~(13)} \wedge \neg \mathsf{Conflict} \wedge \neg \mathsf{Exh}] \le Q_H \cdot P_{\mathrm{UB}}(48, q_s)$. Summing Steps~1--4 proves \eqref{eq:euf-cma-rom}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Concrete interpretation of \eqref{eq:euf-cma-rom}]
At $q_s = 2^{48}$, the $V$-space terms evaluate to $q_s(q_s - 1)/(2\alpha p_{\mathrm{cap}} 2^{256}) \approx 2^{-147.8}$ and $q_s Q_H / (\alpha p_{\mathrm{cap}} 2^{256}) \approx Q_H / 2^{194.8}$, while the $256$-bit quadratic terms sum to $\le 2 Q_H^2 / 2^{256}$ for $Q_H \gg q_s$. With $P_{\mathrm{UB}}(48, 2^{48}) \le 2^{-131.7076}$, the linear query term is $\approx 2.0765\, Q_H / 2^{128}$. Ignoring PRF terms, the upper bound \eqref{eq:euf-cma-rom} reaches advantage $1/2$ at $Q_H \approx 2^{125.6}$ whole-input random-oracle queries---a necessary-work lower bound implied by our proof, distinct from both concrete attack cost (measured in \BLAKE{} compressions) and the $131.7$-bit \UPORS{} component bound.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Packed \WOTSC{} vs.\ Zero-Table Sperner DP on ARM Cortex-M33}

We evaluated embedded behavior using a bare-metal \texttt{thumbv8m.main-\allowbreak none-\allowbreak eabihf} harness under QEMU~11.1.3 (\texttt{-machine mps2-an505 -cpu cortex-m33}) with a custom TCG Thumb-2 instruction-class counter and byte-exact \texttt{0xA5A5A5A5} SRAM sentinel watermark probing (measuring overwritten stack memory). \emph{We explicitly distinguish exact QEMU-executed Thumb-2 instruction counts and sentinel watermarks from Arm Cortex-M33 TRM~\cite{arm2020m33trm} $0$-wait-state cycle/time estimates at $160\text{ MHz}$; physical Cortex-M33 silicon has not been measured.}

Streaming \texttt{TreeHash} watermarks measure $996\text{ B}$ for a height-$7$ lower tree and $636\text{ B}$ for the height-$11$ \UPORS{} tree.

\subsection{Thumb-2+FPU \BLAKE{} Assembly and $32$-Byte \texttt{CHAIN} Layout}
Because $97.0\%$ of ideal fixed warm-signing compressions with the $16\text{ KiB}$ partial cut ($944{,}184 / 973{,}574$) and $95.8\%$ of short-message verification compressions ($3{,}603 / 3{,}761$) are standard $32$-byte \texttt{CHAIN} calls, we implemented an ARMv8-M Thumb-2+FPU \texttt{CHAIN} kernel using deferred barrel-shifter rotation fusion (\texttt{add.w}/\texttt{eor.w} with inline \texttt{ror \#7/\#8/\#12/\#16}, removing all $224$ explicit rotate instructions/compression) and holding compression state in FPU registers (\texttt{s0..s31}, $0.2\%$ memory instructions). Differentially checked against \texttt{blake3::keyed\_hash} across a $59$-step test chain, this kernel executes \textbf{$653.2$ Thumb-2 instructions/compression} ($1.83\times$ fewer instructions than stock general-purpose Rust \texttt{blake3::keyed\_hash}'s $1{,}193.4$ instructions; TRM-modeled $\approx 655$ vs.\ $\approx 1{,}459$ $0$-wait cycles, $2.23\times$). On the TRM $160\text{ MHz}$ model with the $16\text{ KiB}$ partial cut ($973{,}574$ ideal harvested fixed + $34{,}019$ mean / $75{,}533$ $p99$ grind), warm signing models at $\approx 4.44\text{ s}$ mean / $\approx 4.82\text{ s}$ $p99$ (with ASM on \texttt{CHAIN} only and stock \texttt{blake3} elsewhere; $\approx 4.39\text{ s}$ $p99$ with ASM throughout; $\approx 9.57\text{ s}$ $p99$ in stock \texttt{blake3}) and short-message verification models at $\approx 16.2\text{ ms}$.

We also tested an alternative $52$-byte Round-$0$-skip \texttt{CHAIN} layout (\texttt{m[8..11]=val, m[12]=step}): while it reduced modeled Cortex-M33 cycles by $6.1\%$ ($\approx 655 \to 615$), it exited \texttt{blake3-servil-core}'s $32$-byte packed SIMD path and regressed Apple~M6 signing by $+37\%$ and verification by $+50\%$, motivating our choice to keep the standard $32$-byte \texttt{CHAIN} layout.

\subsection{Desktop Single-Threaded SIMD and Proved Zero-False-Negative Optimizations}
In \texttt{signet} (strictly single-threaded \texttt{blake3-servil-core = "=0.11.0"}):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{$19{,}584$-chain batch wave:} Signing batches all $5 \cdot 128 \cdot 21 = 13{,}440$ lower-hypertree chains ($59$ steps) together with all $2{,}048 \cdot 3 = 6{,}144$ \UPORS{} chains ($23$ steps, retired early after step $23$) into $612$ groups of $32$ chains with zero partial batches (regenerating active lower-layer chain values in a second tiny pass only for the single winning leaf per layer, avoiding retaining $19{,}584 \times 60$ intermediate states).
  \item \textbf{Exact SWAR \WOTSC{} bounds test (\texttt{src/coding.rs}):} Writing $w_0 = \mathrm{LE64}(H_\ell[0..8])$ and $w_1 = \mathrm{LE64}(H_\ell[8..16])$ with $(w_0 \bmod 4) = 0$, the first $10$ packed $6$-bit digits reside in $a = w_0 \gg 2$, digit $10$ is $d_{10} = (w_0 \gg 62) + ((w_1 \mathbin{\&} \mathtt{0xf}) \ll 2)$, and the remaining $10$ digits reside in $b = w_1 \gg 4$. Because a $6$-bit integer $d \in [0, 64)$ satisfies $d \ge 60$ ($\mathtt{111100}_2..\mathtt{111111}_2$) \emph{if and only if} bits $2,3,4,5$ are all $1$, setting $M = \mathtt{0x0104\_1041\_0410\_4104}$ (bit $2$ of lanes $0..9$) gives the exact branchless predicate:
    \begin{align*}
      \big(a \mathbin{\&} (a \gg 1) \mathbin{\&} (a \gg 2) \mathbin{\&} (a \gg 3) \mathbin{\&} M\big) &= 0, \qquad d_{10} < 60, \\
      \big(b \mathbin{\&} (b \gg 1) \mathbin{\&} (b \gg 2) \mathbin{\&} (b \gg 3) \mathbin{\&} M\big) &= 0.
    \end{align*}
  \item \textbf{Proved zero-false-negative pure-$u64$ bit-fold \UPORS{} prefilter (\texttt{src/few\_time.rs}):} Let $S_0$ be the set of distinct leaves appearing in the first $9$ $11$-bit chunks of $H_{\mathrm{sel}}$. Whenever $H_{\mathrm{sel}}$ yields $9$ distinct leaves $S$ within $18$ chunks, we necessarily have $S_0 \subseteq S$. Because subtree occupancy $n_j(\cdot)$ is monotone under inclusion ($n_j(S) \ge n_j(S_0)$ for all $j \in \{1,\dots,10\}$) and non-increasing in height ($n_j(S) \ge n_5(S) \ge n_5(S_0)$ for all $j \in \{1,\dots,5\}$),
    \[
      \mathrm{sibs}(S) + 7 \;=\; \sum_{j=1}^{10} n_j(S) \;\ge\; 5\, n_5(S_0) + \sum_{j=6}^{10} n_j(S_0) \;=:\; \mathrm{lb}(S_0).
    \]
    Setting bit $\lfloor x / 32 \rfloor \in [0, 64)$ of a single $64$-bit mask $m_5$ for each of the first $9$ draws computes $n_5(S_0) = \mathrm{popcnt}(m_5)$ and $n_6(S_0),\dots,n_{10}(S_0)$ via $5$ masked bit-folds. Rejecting whenever $\mathrm{lb}(S_0) > 52$ is \emph{provably exact} (zero false negatives on all $2^{256}$ digests, preserving the exact minimum $\mathrm{ctr}_{\mathrm{ft}}$), filters $99.30\%$ of non-qualifying digests without sorting, and cuts desktop \UPORS{} grinding $20.1\times$ ($3.27\text{ ms} \to 163\;\mu\text{s}$).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Quantum Security, Multi-User Scope, and Open Problems}
\label{sec:quantum}
Our headline $128$-bit target is \emph{classical single-user} security. In abstract quantum random-oracle (QROM) query units, a single $128$-bit preimage target has query scale $O(2^{64})$, arbitrary collisions in a $128$-bit output have BHT query scale $O(2^{128/3})$ (noting that arbitrary collisions do not automatically yield root-anchored second preimages or signature forgeries), and collisions in the public $256$-bit content hash have BHT query scale $O(2^{256/3})$. As Daniel~J.~Bernstein proved in his SHARCS~2009 physical cost analysis of hash collisions~\cite{bernstein2009sharcs}, however, counting abstract oracle queries omits the 2D/3D hardware area--time and quantum-RAM routing costs of a cryptographically relevant quantum computer (CRQC): on a physical 2D mesh, BHT's $O(2^{n/3})$ coherent-memory routing latency leaves total hardware$\times$time collision cost no better than classical parallel Pollard-$\rho$ search. For preimage search across $M$ parallel processors, amplitude amplification reduces serial depth to $O(\sqrt{N/M})$ with hardware$\times$time product scaling as $\sqrt{MN}$ (requiring large serial depth for small $M$, approaching $N$ as $M \to N$, and incurring fault-tolerant gate overhead). While \BLINCS{} relies on no Shor-vulnerable algebraic structure, formal QROM security proofs for adaptive \WOTSC{}/\UPORS{} grinding (cf.~\cite{don2022qrom}) remain open.

\paragraph{Multi-user security and open work.}
\Cref{thm:euf-cma-rom} covers single-user security only. Across $U$ independent users, the $128$-bit $\mathsf{pk\_seed}$ admits key-sharing coincidences with small-probability union bound $U(U-1)/2^{129}$ (in addition to generic multi-user advantage amplification across distinct keys). Tight multi-user proofs, QROM lifting, direct matched-parameter implementations comparing \BLINCS{} against \textsf{PORS+FP}~\cite{abri2025porsfp}, \textsf{BPORS+FP}~\cite{ren2026bporsfp}, and \textsf{BPORSC}~\cite{wang2026bporsc}, and physical Cortex-M33 silicon measurements with DPA/FI hardening remain open work.
